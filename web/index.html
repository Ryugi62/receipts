How do we know it reads the evidence?

Off-the-shelf language-inference models have a known shortcut: they often call an unrelated sentence a "contradiction". A naive fact-checker would then tell you a true fact is false. Receipts adds a relevance gate and we measured it on public, human-labelled data:

    Use the Swap test on any claim to see it yourself: we feed the claim an unrelated sentence and the verdict must become "no receipt".

    What Receipts is not

    Wikipedia is not the truth, and "no receipt" does not mean false — it means "go check this one". Receipts uses pretrained models only (no training) and the thresholds were chosen on separate development data. Full method, numbers and misses: docs/eval.md.
